=== IMPORTANT: Seed the GTFS database on first deploy ===

The GTFS timetable (gtfs.db) lives on the PVC and is refreshed weekly by the
gtfs-refresh CronJob. The PVC starts empty on a fresh deploy, so the live
board will show nothing until the first import completes.

Trigger the one-off seed immediately after the first deploy:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} create job --from=cronjob/{{ include "bustracker.fullname" . }}-gtfs-refresh gtfs-seed

This takes ~30–60 minutes on a Raspberry Pi (8.6M stop_time rows).
The app will show an empty board during this time — that is expected, not a bug.
Once done, the weekly CronJob keeps the timetable current automatically.
